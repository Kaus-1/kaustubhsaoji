Spiking neural networks (SNNs) process information as discrete events, which makes them a natural fit for the spatio-temporal structure of EEG and attractive for low-power, always-on monitoring.

- Trained SNNs on EEG datasets using **spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP)** and **surrogate-gradient** methods.
- Designed **temporal coding schemes** to capture the spatio-temporal features in EEG signals that matter for seizure prediction.
- Benchmarked the **event-driven efficiency** of SNNs against conventional deep-learning models.

The broader question I like here is the same one that shows up in protein design: how to get reliable predictions out of models that are cheap enough to run at scale.
