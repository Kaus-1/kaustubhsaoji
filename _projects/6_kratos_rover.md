As Science Lead, I led a team of 50+ members on the astrobiology and autonomy side of a competition Mars rover.

- Designed and ran **chemical tests for life-detection** experiments carried out by the rover.
- Built **machine-learning pipelines for autonomous rock detection and classification** to support planetary-exploration tasks.
- Mentored team members across disciplines and coordinated the science payload with the wider engineering effort.

The team placed **7th in the world at the International Rover Challenge 2025**. Leading a large, cross-disciplinary group toward a hard deadline is some of the most useful training I've had for research.
