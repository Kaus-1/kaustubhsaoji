This wet-lab project studied how the transcriptome varies across developmental stages of the horseshoe crab, which required clean, high-yield RNA from a difficult sample.

- Developed a **modified CTAB method** that achieved an RNA yield of **300 ng/µL** from *Tachypleus gigas*, outperforming commercial kits.
- Reached an **A₂₆₀/A₂₈₀ ratio of ~2.0**, indicating high purity and effective protein-free extraction.
- Increased total RNA yield by roughly **5× over the standard TRIzol protocol**, enabling reliable downstream applications.

It was my first taste of how much careful protocol work determines whether the interesting downstream science is even possible — a lesson that carries straight into design-and-test loops.
