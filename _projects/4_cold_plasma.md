During a summer research internship at CSIR-CEERI, I worked on cold atmospheric plasma (CAP) as a non-invasive approach to treating skin cancer.

- Co-authored the review **"Harnessing cold atmospheric plasma for skin cancer: a non-invasive breakthrough in plasma medicine,"** published in *Drug Discovery Today* ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drudis.2025.104491)).
- Investigated apoptotic and molecular pathways in skin cancer, comparing conventional therapies against CAP-induced apoptosis.
- Contributed to the design and development of **portable CAP prototype devices** for direct dermatological application.
