This project combines quantum chemistry with structure-based screening to look for drug-repurposing opportunities.

- Optimised molecular geometries and computed electronic properties for molecules from the **QM9 / QM40** datasets using GAMESS and Avogadro.
- Identified structural overlaps between these datasets and **FDA-approved drugs** by matching on SMILES and InChI identifiers, surfacing candidates for repurposing.
- Ran **molecular docking** with AutoDock Vina to compare the binding affinities of FDA-approved antivirals and phytochemicals against viral targets.

The work bridges first-principles property prediction and pragmatic virtual screening — using cheap, well-characterised chemistry to prioritise molecules worth a closer look.
